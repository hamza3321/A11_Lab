    Issue tracker

      Audit report & checklist

      Test-method guide

      • Keyboard only: unplug the mouse. Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, Arrows, Esc. Look for traps, lost focus and invisible focus.
      • Screen readers: NVDA + Firefox or Chrome (Windows), VoiceOver + Safari (macOS/iOS), TalkBack (Android). Check names, roles, states and announcements.
      • Zoom: browser zoom to 200% and 400% (320px wide); OS magnifier; text-spacing bookmarklet.
      • Contrast: a contrast checker or the Module 2 tool; test hover, focus and disabled states too.
      • Automated: axe DevTools, Lighthouse, WAVE. These catch only a portion of WCAG issues (commonly estimated at roughly a third); manual testing is required.

      A11y Lab is a learning aid. It cannot replace testing with real assistive technology and real users.
